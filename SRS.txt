# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

## Weekly Football Stats Tracker

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

The Weekly Football Stats Tracker is a web application designed to record weekly football sessions and automatically maintain player and match statistics.

The application minimizes manual input by requiring only goal events during gameplay while automatically calculating scores, player statistics, and leaderboards.

---

# 1.2 Scope

The application shall provide:

* Player Management
* Session Management
* Team Management
* Goal Recording
* Automatic Score Calculation
* Automatic Player Statistics
* Historical Match Records
* Leaderboards
* Support for Two-Team and Three-Team (Winner Stays) formats

---

# 1.3 Intended Users

* Football Group Members
* Match Organizer (Administrator)

---

# 3. Functional Requirements

---

## FR-01 Player Management

The system shall allow:

* Create Player
* Edit Player
* Delete Player
* Search Player
* Upload Profile Picture (Future)

Player attributes:

* Player ID
* Name
* Jersey Number
* Photo
* Created Date

---

## FR-02 Session Management

The system shall allow administrators to:

Create

* Session Name
* Date
* Location
* Notes
* Mode

Modes:

* Standard Match
* Winner Stays

---

## FR-03 Team Management

For each session the administrator shall:

Create teams.

Mode 1

* Team A
* Team B

Mode 2

* Team A
* Team B
* Team C

Players may belong to only one team per session.

---

## FR-04 Goal Recording

The administrator selects a player.

The system shall automatically:

* Add Goal Event
* Increase Player Goals
* Increase Team Score
* Update Timeline
* Refresh Dashboard

No manual score input is required.

---

## FR-05 Winner Stays Rotation

Applicable only in Winner Stays mode.

After each goal:

The system shall:

Determine scoring player's team.

That team remains.

The waiting team enters.

The losing team becomes the waiting team.

Rotation updates automatically.

---

## FR-06 No Goal Override

The administrator may trigger:

"No Goal / Time Up"

The system shall:

* Record timeout event
* Rotate teams
* Preserve current winning team
* Leave all player statistics unchanged

---

## FR-07 Undo

The system shall support:

Undo Last Event

The system recalculates:

* Score
* Statistics
* Timeline
* Rotations

---

## FR-08 End Session

When ending the session:

The system shall:

Lock session.

Calculate:

* Final score
* Winning team
* Games Played
* Player statistics

Store permanently.

Primary entities

* Players
* Sessions
* Teams
* TeamPlayers
* GoalEvents
* SessionEvents

The GoalEvents table shall be treated as the primary source of statistical calculations.

---

# 7. Business Rules

BR-01

A player may belong to only one team during a session.

---

BR-02

Each goal increments:

* Player Goals
* Team Score

---

BR-03

Game score is automatically generated.

---

BR-04

Manual score editing is not permitted.

---

BR-05

Winner Stays mode automatically rotates teams after every goal.

---

BR-06

No Goal override shall not affect player statistics.

---

BR-07

Ended sessions become read-only.

---

# 8. Constraints

* Internet connection required (v1)
* One active session at a time
* Administrator permissions required for editing

---

# 9. Assumptions

* Player list exists before session creation.
* Every goal belongs to one player.
* A player cannot score for two teams in the same session.
* Sessions cannot change game mode after starting.
