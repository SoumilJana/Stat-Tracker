StatTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Welcome to StatTracker. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your data is collected and used.

1. Information We Collect

When you use StatTracker, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Details: Name, username, and email address used for registration and authentication.
  • Match & Gameplay Statistics: Goals, assists, match participation, team assignments, and season awards recorded during matches.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Web push subscription endpoints and public encryption keys used exclusively to deliver match announcements, schedules, and post-game statistics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute and display live match leaderboards, player cards, overall ratings, and Hall of Fame awards.
  • To send scheduled match reminders and match outcome notifications.
  • To authenticate and authorize player and admin actions within the app.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is securely stored and encrypted in transit using PostgreSQL and Supabase cloud infrastructure with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You can view and update your profile details directly in the app. If you wish to permanently delete your account or data, you may contact the administrator or reach out to us at support@stattracker.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@stattracker.com.
